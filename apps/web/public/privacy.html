Privacy Policy

Isles of Merchants (webri.co) · Last updated 29 October 2026

Isles of Merchants is an online board game. You can play as a guest without an account. This page explains what we store and how to remove it.

Playing as a guest

  • We store the nickname you type, your seats in game rooms and the moves played, so a game can continue and be reviewed.
  • Your browser keeps a seat key, your language and your game settings in local storage. We do not use cookies for this.

Signing in (optional)

  • Sign-in is handled by Supabase. If you sign in with an email code, Supabase stores your email address. If you sign in with Google, Supabase receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Our game server receives only your account ID. It links that ID to your game seats so you can find and continue your games on any device. We do not copy your email address into our game database.
  • We use your Google name only to suggest a nickname. We do not use your Google data for anything else, and we do not sell or share it.

Analytics

We count daily visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or track individual visitors.

How long we keep data

Rooms are deleted automatically after they have been empty for about a day. Account links last until you delete your account.

Deleting your data

Sign in on the lobby page and choose Delete account. This removes your account from Supabase and unlinks your seats; your past games stay as anonymous guest seats. You can also simply sign out and clear the site's data in your browser.
